# Java QCM — Design System (unslop)

Credit: direction and craft rules follow [heydamir/unslop](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop) by Damir Sokolovsky (@heydamir), CC BY 4.0.

## Direction

- **Product.** Offline Java interview QCM for candidates practicing and sitting timed mocks on their own device.
- **Character.** Exact, quiet, exam-room.
- **World.** Graphite desk under a warm lamp — paper, pencil, answer sheet.
- **Moments.** (1) Timer and score as the one bright mono figure. (2) Correct / incorrect answer material as the only colored content.
- **Signature.** Physical keycaps for answer shortcuts (`1`–`9`, `Enter`, `F`).

## Tokens

Graphite ramp from unslop `tokens.md`. Accent amber from the lamp world (`#b45309` light / `#fbbf24` dark). Semantic green / red only for answer states.

Type: IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Mono. Weights 400–500. Sentence case. No uppercase tracked labels.

Spacing: 4px grid. Nav rows ~36. Panel radius 12, controls 8. Hairlines at ~8% ink.

Motion: one ease `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`. Hover = surface change. Press = scale 0.98. No entrance stagger on chrome.
